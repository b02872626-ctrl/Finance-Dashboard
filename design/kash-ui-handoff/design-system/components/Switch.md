An on/off switch for settings that apply immediately, such as tracking a bank.

**You provide:** a `label` (accessible name, e.g. "Track Telebirr"), and `checked` + `onChange` or `defaultChecked`.

- Place it at the right end of a list row inside a card; the row's text names what it controls.
- On is slate; off is `line-strong`.
- A switch acts at once. If the change needs confirming, use a button instead.
