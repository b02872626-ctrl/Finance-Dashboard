A 44px square icon-only button for back, settings, add, share, export and calendar.

**You provide:** `icon`, a `label` (its accessible name, required), `tone` and `href` or `onClick`.

- `tone="light"` on surface: white with a hairline. `tone="onSlate"`: a translucent ghost on slate.
- `size="l"` (52px) sits beside a compact `Button` in the hero, as on Home.
- Back always sits top-left; one or two utilities sit top-right.

**Don't** put text in an icon button or use one for the screen's main action.
