How Kash handles
your money data

  • Only bank messagesKash reads SMS from banks and mobile money. Personal chats are never opened.

  • Who can see itYou. The Kash team can also access synced data to run and fix the service. It is never sold or shared.
  • On this phoneA copy stays on your phone, so Kash works without internet.
