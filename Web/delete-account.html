Delete your account

Kash · Developer: Nahusenay Gebreamlak

The short version
  • Delete your account from inside the app, or email us and we will do it for you.
  • Everything synced to your Kash account is deleted straight away: transactions, bank messages, categories, budgets, goals and sessions.
  • Nothing is kept except feedback survey answers, which are unlinked from you.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Kash and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its synced data are removed from our servers, and the copy on your phone is cleared.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email nahusenaygebreamlak@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Kash account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done. We may ask you to confirm from that same email address so nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name and Google profile details if you used Google sign-in).
  • All synced transactions and the bank SMS they were read from.
  • Your categories, category rules, budgets, goals, income entries and review history.
  • Dashboard sessions and device status records.

What is kept

  • Answers you gave in the in-app feedback survey, with the link to your account removed so they are no longer about you.
  • An anonymous install record (a random ID, app version and phone model) that was never tied to your account.

Deleting only some data

To stop Kash reading new bank SMS without deleting your account, revoke the SMS permission in your phone's settings. To remove the data on your phone only, uninstall the app; your synced copy stays until you delete the account.
