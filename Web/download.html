What it asks for, and why

Three permissions. No surprises.

SMS

To read messages from banks and mobile money, like CBE, Telebirr, Awash and Dashen. Other messages are skipped: never stored or uploaded.

Notifications

Alerts for a low balance, money coming in and unusual bank fees, plus one evening summary. Each one can be turned off in the app.

Internet

To keep your transactions in your account, so they're on the web dashboard and safe if you change phones.

Your money, your messages

Is my data safe?

Kash never asks for your bank PIN or password, and can't move money.

  • Which messages does it read?

    Only SMS from the banks and wallets listed above. Messages from people, and anyone else, are skipped.

  • Do my codes leave the phone?

    No. One-time codes, promotions and notices are never uploaded. Only money messages (payments, transfers, balances) are kept.

  • Who can see my transactions?

    They live on your phone first and sync to your account over an encrypted connection. Other users can never see them. Kash's developer can look only to fix an import problem or answer your support request.

  • Is my data sold?

    No. No ads, no selling, no sharing for marketing. Kash is free.

  • Can I delete it?

    Yes, any time: Settings → Delete account removes your account and all its data, straight away.
