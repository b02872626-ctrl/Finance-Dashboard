How to install

It takes a minute.

Finance Lore isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to confirm the install.

  1. 1Tap Download for Android and wait for it to finish.
  2. 2Open the file from your notifications or your Downloads / My Files app.
  3. 3If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.
  4. 4Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. 5Open Finance Lore, sign in, and allow SMS access so it can read your bank messages.

Samsung phones: the setting is under Settings → Apps → ⋮ → Special access → Install unknown apps. You can turn it off again after installing.

What it asks for, and why

Three permissions. No surprises.

SMS

To read messages from banks and mobile-money services like CBE, Telebirr, Awash and Dashen. Other messages are skipped — never stored or uploaded.

Notifications

A reminder to review new transactions. You can turn this off in the app.

Internet

To sync your transactions to your account so they're on the web dashboard and safe if you change phones.

Finance Lore never asks for your bank PIN or password, and can't move money.
